01Who we are

Hainan's only off-site, independent bonded park

Our park is the only off-site, independent bonded park in Hainan Province. Engines and generator sets from overseas enter under customs bond, are repaired or remanufactured here, and ship back out, with no import duty on the equipment.

We hold AEO advanced certification from Haikou Customs, which means faster clearance for every shipment. Our motto: purchase globally, sell globally, repair globally, rent globally and invest globally.
