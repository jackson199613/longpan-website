Remanufactured means rebuilt and proven

A used unit is sold as found. A SeaSpark remanufactured unit is taken apart, rebuilt to our standard and load-tested, with the evidence in your hands.

  1. Inspection and evaluation

    Measure wear on core components and issue a written evaluation report.

  2. Disassembly and cleaning

    Separate engine, alternator and controls; clean and sort reusable parts.

  3. Core component repair

    Precision repair of crankshafts and stators; replace filters, sensors and wear parts.

  4. System and enclosure renewal

    Rust removal and repaint; rework electrical, fuel and oil circuits.

  5. Performance testing

    No-load, step-load and protection tests at 50, 70, 100 and 110% load.

  6. Certification and records

    Remanufacture nameplate, certificate of conformity and an electronic file for each unit.

Questions buyers ask first

Straight answers on bonded repair, remanufactured units and fuel. For anything else, ask an engineer directly.

Ask an engineer
What is bonded repair in Hainan Free Trade Port?

Under Hainan's bonded maintenance policy, equipment from outside China enters the bonded zone under customs supervision, is repaired and re-exported. No import duty is charged on the equipment itself. Hainan Longpan operates the province's first government-approved off-zone bonded repair pilot, approved on 24 August 2025.

Which equipment can you repair under bond?

Marine main engines, diesel and gas generator sets, fracturing-truck and mining-truck engines, and construction machinery powertrains. Send the model and a nameplate photo and we will confirm eligibility before anything ships.

How is a remanufactured generator set different from a used one?

A used set is sold as found. A remanufactured set is fully disassembled, has worn core parts repaired or replaced, and passes load tests at 50, 70, 100 and 110% before it receives a remanufacture nameplate and certificate.

How long does an engine overhaul take?

It depends on the model and its condition. After inspection we give a dated schedule for each stage: entry, teardown, overhaul and re-export. If downtime is critical, ask about shipping an exchange engine first.

Can your gas generator sets run on associated gas or biogas?

Yes. We supply and maintain gas generator sets for associated gas, coalbed methane, biogas, landfill gas and wastewater gas. Send a recent fuel gas analysis so we can match the engine and configuration.
