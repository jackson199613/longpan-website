04Remanufacturing standard

Rebuilt, tested and certified, unit by unit

A used set is sold as found. Every SeaSpark unit is taken apart, rebuilt and proven on our test bench, with the evidence in your hands.

  1. Classification, disassembly and testing

    Components separated, inspected and screened, with a written classification report.

  2. Component repair and refurbishment

    Precision repair of crankshafts and stators; filters, sensors and wear parts replaced.

  3. Reassembly and testing

    Rebuilt to standard, then no-load, step-load and protection tests. Rework if it misses.

  4. Certification and warehousing

    Remanufacture nameplate, certificate and an electronic file for every unit.
